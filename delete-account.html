Account Deletion

Delete your Roster account

Roster: Shift & Pay Tracker · Developed by Kolli Vana Sathya Kumari (Anits) · Last updated: 20 October 2026

You can delete your Roster account and all its data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed. This page explains how to do it, what gets deleted, and when.

Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account does not cancel a Roster Pro subscription, because Apple or Google bills you directly. To stop future charges:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Roster → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Roster → Cancel

1. Choose how to delete

Fastest

In the app

Open Roster → Profile → Delete account, then confirm. Your account goes into pending deletion right away.

No app needed

Request it here

Uninstalled the app or can't sign in? Use the request form below or email us from the address on your account.

Before you delete: export your data

Once your data is permanently deleted, it can't be recovered. If you want to keep your records, export them first in the app as a CSV file.

2. Request deletion without the app

Fill in the form below. It opens a pre-written email in your email app, which you then send to us. Please send it from the email address you use for Roster so we can confirm the account is yours. We never ask for your password.

Please enter a valid email address.

Please tick the box to confirm.

Your email app should now be open with the request ready to send. Press Send to submit it.

If nothing opened, email anitshub@anits.in from your Roster email address with the subject "Roster account deletion request".

We'll confirm by email once your account is in pending deletion, usually within 5 business days. Requests are completed within 30 days at most.

3. What happens next

  • Day 0: Pending deletion Your account is deactivated and you can't use the app, but your data is kept intact.
  • Days 1–30: Grace period Changed your mind? Sign in to Roster and choose to reactivate. All your data is restored immediately.
  • Day 30: Permanent deletion All your personal data is permanently deleted from our live systems and cannot be recovered.
  • Day 90: Anti-abuse record removed The last record (an irreversible hash of your email address plus your device identifier, kept only to prevent repeat free trials) is deleted. After this you're free to create a new Roster account with the same email address.

4. What we delete and what we keep

Deleted after 30 days

  • Name and email address
  • Country / region and profile settings
  • All shifts, jobs and pay rates
  • Earnings and payment-status history
  • Subscription status linked to your account
  • Promo code redemptions (Android)

Kept temporarily, then deleted

  • Up to 90 days: one-way SHA-256 hash of your email and your device identifier, used only to prevent free-trial abuse. The hash cannot be turned back into your email.
  • Encrypted backups: overwritten on our normal backup cycle and never used for anything else.
  • Transaction references: kept only where tax or accounting law requires it.

Payment records are held by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall Roster.

5. Delete some data without closing your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific information:

  • Shifts: open a shift in the app and delete it.
  • Jobs: delete a job from your jobs list. You'll be asked what to do with its shifts.
  • Profile details: edit your name or country in Profile.
  • Anything else: email us and say what you'd like removed.
