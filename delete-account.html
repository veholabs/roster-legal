Account Deletion

Delete your account

Last updated: 1 June 2025 · Applies to the Roster app (com.anits.rosterapp)

You can delete your Roster account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what happens to your data.

1. How to delete your account

The quickest way is directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Roster app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right tab).
  3. Scroll to Account → Delete account.
  4. Follow the prompts and confirm.

If you no longer have access to the app — for example, you've uninstalled it or can't sign in — email us at legal@anits.in from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We action verified requests within 30 days.

2. 30-day grace period

30-day grace period: When you delete your account it is first scheduled for deletion and enters a pending state. Your data remains intact and you can reactivate your account simply by signing in at any time during this window — nothing is lost.

After 30 days: All personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers, including shifts, jobs, earnings history, name, and profile information.

3. What gets deleted

After the 30-day grace period, the following are permanently deleted:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile (name, country)
  • All jobs, shifts, and earnings history
  • All settings and preferences

4. What is kept, and for how long

Anti-abuse retention: A one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hash of your email address is retained for up to 90 days after deletion, used solely to prevent free trial abuse. The hash cannot be reversed to obtain your email address. After 90 days this record is also permanently deleted.

DataRetentionReason
Account & all personal dataDeleted after 30-day graceAccount closure
Hashed email (non-reversible)Up to 90 daysPrevent repeated free-trial abuse

You are free to create a new Roster account with the same email address after this 90-day period.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details on how we handle your data.

Questions about account deletion?

Contact us at legal@anits.in or visit anits.in. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
