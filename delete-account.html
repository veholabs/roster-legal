Delete your account

Applies to the Roster app (package com.anits.rosterapp).

You can delete your Roster account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, what happens to your data, and how long anything is kept.

How to delete your account

The quickest way is directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Roster app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right tab).
  3. Scroll to Account → Delete account.
  4. Follow the prompts and confirm.

If you no longer have access to the app (for example, you've uninstalled it or can't sign in), email us at veholabs@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We'll action verified requests within 30 days.

⏳ 30-day grace period. When you delete your account it is first scheduled for deletion. For 30 days you can restore everything simply by logging back in — nothing is lost during this window. After 30 days, your account and data are permanently removed.

What gets deleted

After the 30-day grace period, the following are permanently deleted:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile (name, country)
  • All jobs, shifts, and earnings history
  • All settings and preferences

What is kept, and for how long

To prevent abuse of free trials, we retain a limited record after deletion. No readable personal data is kept — only a one-way hashed form of your email address, which cannot be used to contact you or reconstruct your account.

DataRetentionReason
Account & all personal data Deleted after 30-day grace Account closure
Hashed email (non-reversible) Up to 90 days Prevent repeated free-trial abuse

After 90 days, the hashed record is removed as well. You are free to create a new Roster account with the same email address after that period.

Questions

For any questions about account deletion or your data, contact veholabs@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
