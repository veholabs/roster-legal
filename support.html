Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Roster Pro subscription on a new phone?

Sign in to Roster with the same account, open the Roster Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so they are cancelled there:

  • iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Roster → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Roster → Cancel

You keep Roster Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

How long is the free trial?

New accounts get a 14-day free trial with every feature. After that, you can continue with a monthly or yearly Roster Pro subscription.

My earnings look wrong. What should I check?
  • The job's base hourly rate and currency (Profile → your job).
  • The penalty rates for Saturday, Sunday, public holidays and overtime on that job.
  • The unpaid break and which pay conditions are switched on for the shift.
  • Whether multiple rates are set to Highest wins or Multiply together.

Roster gives estimates to help you keep track. Your payslip from your employer is the official record.

A public holiday is missing or shows for the wrong country

Each job uses the public holidays of its own country. Check the job's country (Profile → your job), then open Profile → Public holidays, pick that country and sync. You can also add custom holidays for local or regional days.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Profile → Notifications and check that notifications are on. Then make sure notifications for Roster are allowed in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Roster). Focus or Do Not Disturb can also hold reminders back.

How do I export my shifts?

Go to Profile → Export data, choose a date range and export a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

I forgot my App Lock passcode or can't sign in

If you signed up with email, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. If you used Sign in with Apple or Google, sign in the same way again. If you're still stuck, email us from the address on your account.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that first.
