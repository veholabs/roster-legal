2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address: used to create your account, sign in, and send account emails such as confirmation and password reset.
  • First and last name: used to personalise the app.
  • Country / region: used to look up public holidays and set default pay-rate and currency settings.
  • Shift, job and earnings data: shift dates and times, hours, breaks, job names, pay rates, penalty rates, earnings and payment status that you enter.
  • Promo codes (Android only): if you redeem a promo code in the Android app, we record the code and when you redeemed it. Promo codes can't be redeemed in the iOS app.
  • Messages to us: if you email us, we keep the correspondence so we can help you.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier: an app-specific technical ID for your device. It is linked to your account and is used to prevent abuse of the free trial (one trial per person) and to keep your sessions working. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Subscription information: when you subscribe to Roster Pro, the Apple App Store or Google Play tells us which plan you bought, its status, its renewal and expiry dates, and a transaction reference. We never receive your card or bank details.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your phone number, physical address, location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics, crash-tracking or advertising software.

4. Where your data is stored and how it's protected

  • On your device: your shifts are kept in a local database inside the app's private storage so the app works offline. This storage is protected by your device's built-in encryption and screen lock.
  • In the cloud: your data is synced to Supabase, which runs on Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region in India. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Sign-in session: your sign-in session is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't access and which is protected by your device's built-in encryption. Your App Lock setting is kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • Passwords: we never store your password in readable form. It is stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash.
  • Access controls: database rules ensure each account can access only its own data. We access account data only when needed to provide support you ask for, to investigate abuse or a security issue, or when the law requires it.

International transfers. Your account and shift data is stored in India. If you live elsewhere, this means your data is processed in a country other than your own. Account emails are sent through Resend, which may process your email address outside India, including in the United States. Where data protection law requires it (for example for users in the EU or UK), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in our data processing agreements with Supabase and Resend.

No system is perfectly secure, but we follow industry-standard practices and review them regularly.

5. Third-party services

We share data only with these service providers, and only as much as they need to do their job:

  • Supabase (Privacy Policy): database, sign-in and cloud sync. Supabase processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Resend (Privacy Policy): delivers account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset. Resend receives only your email address and the content of those emails, and processes them on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Apple App Store (Privacy Policy): processes subscription payments on iOS. Apple is responsible for your payment details.
  • Google Play Billing (Privacy Policy): processes subscription payments on Android. Google is responsible for your payment details.
  • Nager.Date: a public API that provides public holiday data. We request holidays by country code only. No personal data is sent.

We may also disclose data if required by law, court order or a valid request from a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or ourselves. If Roster is ever sold or transferred to another owner, your data would transfer under this policy and we would tell you first.

6. Device permissions, notifications and cookies

  • Notifications: if you allow them, Roster sends shift reminders. These are scheduled on your device. They are not marketing, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint (App Lock): if you turn on App Lock, your device's operating system checks your biometrics. Roster never receives, stores or sends your biometric data. It only gets a "success" or "fail" result.
  • No tracking: Roster does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, so it does not ask for tracking permission.
  • Cookies: the Roster app does not use cookies. This legal website is a static page and does not set cookies, so no cookie consent is needed.

7. Account deletion and data retention

You can delete your account in either of these ways:

  • In the app: go to Profile → Delete account.
  • Without the app: use our account deletion page or email anitshub@anits.in from the email address on your account.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so please cancel in your Apple ID settings (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) to stop future charges.

30-day grace period: your account is placed in a pending-deletion state and you cannot use the app. Your data stays intact, and you can reactivate the account and restore everything by signing in again during this period.

After 30 days: all your personal data is permanently deleted from our live systems, including your shifts, jobs, earnings history, name, email, country and profile.

Anti-abuse record (up to 90 days): a one-way SHA-256 hash of your email address, together with your device identifier, is kept for up to 90 days after deletion. It is used only to stop the same person from repeatedly creating accounts to get new free trials. The hash cannot be turned back into your email address.

After 90 days: these remaining records are permanently deleted as well.

Backups and records we must keep: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days until those backups are overwritten. Backups are not used for any other purpose. Purchase and transaction records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it. Data on your own device is removed when you uninstall the app.

While your account is active, we keep your data for as long as you use Roster.

8. Your rights

You can do the following at any time, most of it directly in the app:

  • Access and portability: export your shift data as a CSV file from the app, or ask us for a copy of all the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: edit your shifts, jobs and profile in the app, or ask us to correct anything else.
  • Deletion: delete your account as described in section 7.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent (for example, notifications), you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Grievance and nomination (India): raise a grievance with us using the contact details below and, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Complain to a regulator: if you're unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In India this is the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in the UK, the ICO; and in Australia, the OAIC. We'd appreciate the chance to fix things first.

To make a request, email anitshub@anits.in. We may need to confirm it's really you, and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Data breaches

If a security incident affects your personal data, we will act quickly to contain it. We will notify the relevant authorities within the time the law requires (for example, within 72 hours under GDPR, and to the Data Protection Board of India under Indian law). We will also tell affected users without undue delay what happened, what data was involved, and what steps to take.

10. Age requirement

Roster is a work tool for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
